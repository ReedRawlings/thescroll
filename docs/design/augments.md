# Ability Augmentation

Numbers: [tuning.md](../tuning.md)

> Potentially one of the strongest systems in the game, and one of the least specified.

## Levelling vs. augmenting

Two distinct axes, deliberately separated:

- **Levelling an ability** makes it stronger and **opens new augment slots**.
- **Augmenting an ability** changes *what it does*.

An ability starts with **1 augment slot**.

## What augments can change

| Axis | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Raise status chance up from the base 0%. Higher-rarity augments give higher chances. |
| **Speed class** | Shift an ability between Fast, Normal and Slow — trading speed against effect, per [combat-timing.md](combat-timing.md) §4.3 |
| **Targeting** | Extend to Branched or All |
| **Damage type** | `OPEN` — mentioned in the notes with no rules attached |

## Rarity

**Bronze, Silver, Gold.** Higher rarity gives stronger effects — for status augments, a higher chance to apply.

What rarity means for non-status augments is `OPEN`.

## Combination rules

**No combinations are banned to start.** This is a deliberate opening position: see what players build before restricting anything.

Worth watching — a speed-class augment interacts directly with the §4.3 balance rule. An augment that makes an ability Faster without reducing its effect breaks that rule by construction, since the rule assumes slower means stronger.

## Open

- **Replacement behaviour.** What happens when slotting an augment into a full ability — is the old one destroyed, returned, or swapped? Named in the original notes and never answered.
- **Damage-type modification** rules.
- What rarity changes for augments that are not status augments.
- How many augment slots an ability can reach at maximum level.
- Whether augments are found in the tower, bought from the Oracle, or both.
- Whether augments are permanent once slotted and whether they survive death.
- Whether Familiar innate abilities can be augmented.
- Whether two augments on one ability can conflict.
