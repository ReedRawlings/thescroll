# Relics

Numbers: [tuning.md](../tuning.md) · Terms: [glossary.md](../glossary.md)

> **Status:** new system. Scope is decided; everything else is open.

## What they are

A **relic** is a party-wide boon found in a **chest** on a tower floor, typically placed down a detour so that reaching it costs rooms. Relics affect the whole party rather than a single character, which distinguishes them from equipment (player-only, slot-based) and from augments (ability-specific).

## Lifespan

**Relics are climb-only.** They are lost when the climb ends, whether the player extracts cleanly, clears floor 40, or dies.

This is deliberate:

- Relics can be tuned strong, because nothing compounds across entries.
- Each climb develops its own character from the relics it happens to find.
- They do not become a fifth permanent progression track competing with the four town pillars.

## Relationship to other systems

| System | Scope | Applies to | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Relic** | Climb only | Whole party | Chests on the floor |
| **Equipment** | Permanent on extraction | Player only | Blacksmith, drops |
| **Augment** | Permanent | One ability | Oracle, drops |
| **Item** | Permanent on extraction | Consumed on use | Drops, shops |

Relics do **not** occupy inventory slots. They are not lootable, droppable or tradeable, and they cannot be secured.

## Open

- The relic list. Nothing is designed yet.
- Whether relics stack, and whether duplicates are possible.
- Whether there is a cap on how many a party can hold.
- Whether relics can carry a downside (a cost or drawback alongside the boon).
- Whether relic rarity exists, and whether it ties to depth.
- Whether any relic interacts with floor generation itself (revealing layouts, altering spawn rates, affecting aggro).
- Whether a chest offers a choice of several relics, or grants one.
- Whether relic chests are visually distinct from item chests before opening — this decides whether the detour is an informed decision or a gamble.
