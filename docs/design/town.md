# Town

The permanent-progression hub between climbs.

## Principle

The town must not become an information-dense RPG menu. Each location owns **one narrow purpose**, and those purposes do not overlap. The player should learn the whole town as four words:

> **Leathersmith = carrying · Oracle = abilities · Hatchery = Familiars · Blacksmith = equipment**

Because the player character relevels from 1 on every entry (see [party.md](party.md)), these four pillars are the **entire** permanent progression surface of the game. Their upgrade curves are the real difficulty curve — not enemy stats.

## The locations

### Leathersmith — carrying and protection

Improves the inventory. Sells:

- Additional **backpack slots**
- **Secure slots**, which protect their contents from being lost on a wipe

Secure slots are the Leathersmith's signature offering and the player's answer to loss aversion. See [items-inventory.md](items-inventory.md).

### Oracle — abilities and information

Two branches:

- **Ability development** — permanent ability changes and augments. See [augments.md](augments.md).
- **Information** — limited uses per climb to reveal what a floor holds: the layout, where the stairs are, chest contents, or what is waiting in an unexplored room. See [floor-generation.md](floor-generation.md).

The move to explorable floors gives this branch a much better surface than the old three-option menu did — revealing a *map* is more legible and more valuable than rerolling an abstract choice. It is still blocked on floor generation being tuned.

### Hatchery — Familiars

Hatches eggs recovered from the tower into **permanent** Familiars that join the long-term collection.

This is the pillar the late game runs on. Because the player character falls behind floor level with depth while Familiars keep their levels, Hatchery investment is what makes the summit reachable.

### Blacksmith — equipment

Permanent equipment progression: weapons, shields, and potentially boots. The equipment-focused counterpart to the Leathersmith.

**The least defined of the four.** Direction is set; rules are not.

### Tower — the entrance

Starts a climb. Not an upgrade shop. The player selects which Familiars to bring here.

## Open

- **Blacksmith rules** in full — what it sells, whether it upgrades existing gear or sells new gear, its cost curve and its ceiling.
- **The Oracle upgrade tree** in full, both branches. Blocked on [floor-generation.md](floor-generation.md).
- Whether a general item shop exists separately from the Blacksmith, or the Blacksmith covers consumables too.
- Gold costs across all four pillars, and how they scale — see [economy.md](economy.md).
- Whether town locations unlock progressively or are all available from the start.
- Whether the town has any non-shop content (story, NPCs, quests).
