SHRUGGIE — Shrug



Looks: A tiny body beneath a broad, flattened head, flanked by two enormous upturned hands on thin, bent arms. Its head nestles between the palms, almost cradled by them. Small dark eyes and a crooked, nonchalant mouth echo the ¯\\\_(ツ)\_/¯ expression. At 32×32, the hands and face occupy most of the sprite, with clear gaps between its head and palms.



Why it fits: Its answer to every argument, catastrophe, and impossible question is the same effortless shrug. Nobody knows whether it understands everything or absolutely nothing.



Pose: Head tilted, shoulders raised, palms held beside its cheeks with one slightly higher than the other. Tiny feet planted casually beneath it.



QUIBBLET — 32×32
Design: A mustard-colored ticklike creature with an oversized sideways pincer head attached to a small oval body. Two stout opposing jaws dominate the silhouette, with one squinting eye at their base. A few short gripping legs project from the small body as simple angular clusters. The head occupies roughly two-thirds of the creature’s visible mass.
Pose: Crouched low in three-quarter view, pincer thrust forward and legs braced as if resisting being pulled loose.
Theme: A parasite that feeds on tiny details, clamping onto the smallest flaw and refusing to let go.

NIBLINK — 32×32
Design: A limbless plum creature composed of two thick interlocking jaw loops, with one lime eye between them. The loops themselves form its living body and jaws. Allocate the sprite area to their curved forms and clearly separated openings.
Pose: Hovering in a short forward snapping lunge, with the front loop angled open and the rear loop twisted slightly behind it.

MOULURE — 32×32

Clapcap

Loopip
Looks: A coral-colored creature shaped like a thick, almost-closed ring. Its head continually chases its little white tail around the central hole. Two stubby feet let it shuffle sideways.

Peepod — Small minion
What it looks like: A round, midnight-blue seed creature on two tiny feet. Its split husk reveals one enormous yellow eye. When curious, three bright sprouts pop from its head; when approached, it snaps shut.
Why it fits: Represents lurking: quietly watching everything without joining in. Its little sprouts evoke unread notifications without becoming literal interface icons

Evermaw — Minor boss
What it looks like: A pale, upright salamander with tiny forearms and a huge rectangular mouth extending down its torso. Broad dark bands divide its long tail into chunky sections. During its idle animation, the bands creep toward its mouth, suggesting that its body is continually feeding through itself.
Why it fits: Represents the infinite feed. It consumes whatever catches your interest and produces more of it, becoming an ecosystem you can wander inside without ever reaching an end.

FIZZTEEN
Design: A coral wax creature with a broad five-pointed star for a head, a single burning wick at its upper tip, and a thick body tapering into a curled puddle. One star point droops over its confident grin. Use one gold flame and broad plum shadows.
Pose: Throwing its head backward in a theatrical bow, one thick arm sweeping outward while its melting base curls the opposite way.
Preserve: Star-shaped head, drooping point, and one flame. Keep wax drips to two broad shapes.
Theme: Fifteen minutes of fame. Its performance burns away the body that makes it a star
