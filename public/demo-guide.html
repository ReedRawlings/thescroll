What this build is

A short climb with the full decision loop.

This demo turns the core pitch into one playable circuit: leave a simplified town, cross room-and-corridor floors, meet enemies in the world, resolve contact in a separate battle, and choose whether to climb, extract, or risk a wipe.

It is a three-floor prototype of a planned 40-floor game. It demonstrates the structure and feel; it does not claim the complete campaign, progression economy, or finished content set.

01 · Fieldcraft

How to play

Exploration and combat use different clocks. Move freely through the floor; touching a visible enemy cuts to battle. The combat timeline then handles every turn.

↗

Choose a destination

Tap or click open floor space to move. Choose another point to redirect, or tap the hero to stop.

◎

Enter an encounter

Contact with a visible monster opens the battle view. Enemies can follow through rooms and corridors.

↔

Choose and target

Cycle the focused ability and valid target. Targeting is intentionally manual in this build.

✓

Commit the command

Review the selection, then confirm. The frozen timeline resumes and the action begins charging.

Keyboard support: use ← / → to cycle abilities, ↑ / ↓ to cycle targets, and Enter or Space to confirm. Press F for fullscreen and Esc to pause.

Town

Start from a deliberately compact title hub, review the kit, and bring two persistent Familiars into the tower.

Explore

Cross connected rooms, find stairs and inspect detours in a reproducible generated layout.

Engage

Touch a roaming enemy to shift from real-time exploration to the IP battle timeline.

Climb

Reach the stairs to advance through this demo’s three-floor slice.

End the run

Extract or clear floor three to bank run gold. A wipe loses carried gold, while Familiar levels persist.

04 · Rules ledger

Canon and defaults

The design documentation settles the left column. The right column records choices made so this small prototype can be played and evaluated; those values can change without revising the game’s core promise.

Confirmed game rules

Part of the intended design

  • Party composition is the player plus two Familiars.
  • Exploration is tap-to-move, with visible enemies and no random encounters.
  • Enemy pursuit crosses rooms and corridors; touching one starts a separate battle anywhere walkable.
  • The complete battle simulation pauses while any ally chooses at COM.
  • Targets are selected manually and the command is previewed before commitment.
  • Damage adds ability ATK to character ATK, then applies DEF, level, and type modifiers.
  • A climb ends by extraction, party wipe, or clearing its summit.

Demo implementation defaults

Chosen for this playable slice

  • The prototype climb is three floors rather than the planned 40-floor tower.
  • Each climb receives a recorded seed; reusing it reproduces that room-and-corridor layout.
  • Enemy rosters, room counts, ability values, rewards, and movement tuning are slice-level balance choices.
  • Enemy behavior is a lightweight implementation of the documented first-pass proposal.
  • All demo abilities are single-target. MP is spent on confirmation, dead targets retarget at ACT, and damage has no critical hits or variance.
  • Floor three ends with a 190 HP Warden; the full design instead reserves authored bosses for floors 10, 20, 30, and 40.
  • Normal victories award 18 / 24 / 30 gold by floor; the Warden awards 65. Chests hold 15 / 20 / 25 gold and one draught.
  • Every two victories, living allies gain one demo level, +2 ATK, and up to +4 max HP and current HP. Demo level caps at 3; MP does not recover.
  • The town compresses the long-term hub into a title, saved totals, starting kit, party preview, and tower entrance. No shops are implemented.
  • Each climb begins with two healing draughts, one ether tonic, one escape seed, and ten total slots (three equipment and seven item slots). Each supply occupies its own slot; only currencies stack.
  • Banked gold and Familiar levels persist locally. Actions and a one-second heartbeat save the live climb; backgrounding pauses it for resume. Death still ends it.
Implementation ledger Exact runtime defaults

These values make the three-floor slice deterministic and testable. They document the current build rather than adding rules to the full game specification.

Floor movement

The player moves at 3.3 tiles/s. An alerted enemy chases at 1.65 tiles/s and returns home at 1.1 tiles/s.

A monster detects the player within 3.3 tiles with clear line of sight. It pursues across the floor until separation exceeds 7 tiles, then walks home. Contact below 0.63 tile with no intervening wall begins battle in rooms or corridors. Other enemies retain their positions when battle ends.

The entire 19×25 floor is visible. This slice has no minimap or fog of war.

Enemy decisions

Ordinary enemies choose between their basic and signature moves with seeded weights of 4 : 1, after excluding illegal choices, then select a random living target.

The Warden follows Quick strike → Ember → Crushing blow, falling back to an affordable legal move when needed.

Enemy choices commit at COM and do not react to an unconfirmed player selection.

Commit, target, and preview

MP is paid on confirmation and is never refunded. If a queued target dies before ACT, the move chooses the first living legal unit on the intended side; without one, it ends without effect.

Manual selection initially focuses the first valid target. A preview uses the target’s current stats; intervening actions can change the final result.

Simultaneous ACT arrivals resolve in roster order before simultaneous player COM prompts appear.

Recovery

Mend restores 28 + round(caster ATK × 0.5) HP, capped at missing health.

Outside battle, a healing draught restores up to 40 HP; an ether tonic restores up to 16 MP. Neither revives a fallen ally. MP never regenerates naturally.

Status behavior

Burn deals 4 flat damage, ignoring DEF and type, at the end of each of the target’s next two completed turns. Frost reduces effective SPD by 20% for one completed turn.

Shock lowers DEF by 20% for the next damage instance, including Burn. Stun lasts 1 / 1.5 / 2 simulation seconds for Fast / Normal / Slow sources; COM pauses freeze its timer.

Reapplication refreshes one instance instead of stacking. Different statuses coexist, all clear between battles, and the boss has no status immunity.

Timeline calculation

COM is position 70; ACT is 100. Wait advances at effective SPD × .80 × dt. Charge uses Fast .560, Normal .300, or Slow .226.

Natural SPD is clamped to 1–99. Party level factor is clamped to .70–1.30; enemies use 1.0. Frost multiplies final speed by .80.

05 · Boundaries

What is intentionally absent

The slice favors a complete, inspectable loop over breadth. These systems remain part of the larger design, but this demo does not present them as finished features.

The other 37 floorsThe full tower plans depth bands, authored boss floors, and additional mechanics. This build stops after floor three.
Full town progressionLeathersmith, Oracle, Hatchery, and Blacksmith are long-term pillars. This slice implements no shops.
Complete economyFinal XP curves, drop rates, upgrade costs, inventory expansion, and long-run balance remain tuning work.
Cancel and counterThe timeline exposes charge windows, but interruption rules are still deferred in the design documents.
Boss and deep-floor systemsBoss pacing, traps, micro puzzles, faster pursuers, and forced encounters are beyond this prototype.
Production persistenceThe full design distinguishes town saves from a single live climb. The demo uses only a minimal representation.
AudioThis build has no music or sound effects. Its feedback comes from motion, interface state, effects, and the combat log.
