# Combat demo defaults

`combat.js` is a pure ES module, with no Phaser import. `createParty()` gives hero + two familiars; `createEncounter(floor, boss)` clamps floor to 1–3. `createBattle(party,enemies,seed=1)` retains the provided unit object references (HP/MP carry across fights), resets gauge/status state, and snapshots levels for speed. No MP regeneration occurs.

Call `stepBattle(battle, dtSeconds)` each frame. `phase` is running / command / won / lost. At command, `pendingActorId` identifies a living party member. Call `chooseAbility(battle, abilityId, targetId)`; false means invalid ability, insufficient MP, or invalid target. Multiple simultaneous COM arrivals remain paused until each party member chooses. Extra frame time is discarded at command. Call `previewAction` for valid, damage, healing, chargeSeconds, mpCost, statusChance. Preview reflects present target stats; intervening actions can change the final amount.

Unit UI fields: id/name/side/texture/type/hp/maxHp/mp/maxMp/atk/def/baseSpd/bonus/level/abilities, position (0–100), state (WAIT/COM/CHARGE/DEAD), effectiveSpd, statuses, completedTurns, queued {abilityId,targetId}. Render gauge icons only for living units. `events` retains the most recent 120 structured events, with type, time, actorId where relevant, targetId, amount where relevant, abilityId where relevant, and text. Do not clear events if replay parity matters; render by your own cursor or last event reference.

ABILITIES is keyed by ID. Fields: id,name,tier (Fast/Normal/Slow),type,atk (additive ability ATK),mp,target (enemy/ally),optional heal and augment {status,chance}. All moves are single-target in this first demo. Status is only in explicitly slotted augment data. Quick strike is free for everyone. Exact tier rates .560/.300/.226 and WAIT .80 come from the timing spec. Damage rounds once to nearest integer with a minimum of one. No criticals or damage variance.

Provisional answers to open design questions: MP is paid at confirmation, never refunded. If a queued target dies, choose the first living legal target on the same intended side at ACT. Heal = ability heal (28) + rounded half caster ATK, capped to missing HP. Burn is 4 flat damage ignoring DEF and type, at end of each of two afflicted completed turns. Reapplication refreshes one status instance; different statuses coexist. Frost lasts one completed turn; Shock lowers DEF for the next damage instance, including Burn. Stun lasts 1/1.5/2 simulation seconds by tier; command pauses freeze its timer. No boss immunity. Status clears at the next battle. Simultaneous ACT resolves in roster order before simultaneous COM is exposed. Only a full party wipe loses the battle. Fallen party members remain unavailable until external revival.

Ordinary enemies use seeded weighted legal moves; unaffordable moves and pointless heals are excluded. Boss follows strike → ember → crush, falling back to an affordable move. These are demo defaults implementing the enemy-AI proposal, not settled design canon. Boss HP 190 and floor-3 encounter are a short demo endpoint; the full design's 40-floor progression is not implemented here.

`applyStatus(battle,unit,status,tier='Normal')` is additionally exported for debugging and meaningful tests. `effectiveSpeed(unit,enemyAvgLevel)` and `damage(attacker,defender,ability)` are pure calculations. `TIER_RATES` is exported.
